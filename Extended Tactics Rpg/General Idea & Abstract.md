
an isometric / 2d world to explore with permadeath tactics combat. Broad and abstract build mechanics to promote large diversity between characters and units.

Explorable world with many secrets to uncover and rare items to unlock.

NOTE: Consider a 3D isometric world, and have the sprites be 2d, could be interesting

The early phase of the project is going to be a simple expansion of the capstone project with a larger color palette (imagine jump from gba to snes).
Earliest main goal is to get the game loop working with the open area tactics combat combination.
From there we will work on fleshing out the character, adding the branding and rogue lineage style class tree.
With the class tree comes unit abilities
Most class trees will have their own unique abilities they give, along with some unlockable ones
Along with this we want early equipment implemented

Remember to make it robust from the start

After that point, we will go through the GDD adding all the needed systems and hooking them together, while focusing on my own art abilities on the side.
