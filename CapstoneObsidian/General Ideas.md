
For the placement phase, have your units appear in a list on the left side (special window that sits open just for that)
An arrow points to the top one, and a little text box appears saying place your units.
Next to the current unit, is another window showing their available a abilities. By default the top one is selected, you choose one of them. and that is their ability for the battle. 
When you click on the map, the unit will be placed there and you will move onto your next unit.
Once all of them (up to 4 by the end) are placed the battle will begin.
